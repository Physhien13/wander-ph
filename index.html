About WanderPH Tours

A local travel agency, built around small-group packages

WanderPH Tours is a Philippine-based travel agency specializing in pre-planned, all-inclusive tour packages rather than piecemeal bookings. Each package bundles transport, accommodation, local guides, and activities into one fixed itinerary and price, and is run in partnership with community-based guide cooperatives at every destination — keeping tourism income local and groups small.
